⚠️ Watch out for scammers

Scammers now use artificial intelligence too. They can copy a voice and a face.

This is what a scam looks like

📞A "grandchild" calls and needs money right now.The voice can be fake.
🎬A celebrity in a video promises sure profits.The video is fake.
💬A text with a link, for example a fee for a parcel.Don’t click the link.
🏦The "bank" calls and asks about your account.Hang up.

Never say on the phone

  • PIN
  • password
  • card number
  • text code

Your bank will never ask for your PIN or password.

What to do right away

  1. Hang up. You don’t have to be polite.
  2. Call back on a number you know.
  3. Ask for the family password. A scammer won’t know it.
  4. Don’t pay under pressure. Talk to your family first.
