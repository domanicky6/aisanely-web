Step 1 in more detail

Which assistant should you pick?

One is enough. All of these have a free version. If you can’t decide, pick the one from a company you already use (for example Google or Microsoft).

GChatGPT

By OpenAI. The best known, with lots of guides online. chatgpt.com

✦Gemini

By Google. Handy if you use Gmail, Android or Google Docs. gemini.google.com

CCopilot

By Microsoft. Built into Windows and Office (Word, Outlook). copilot.microsoft.com

AClaude

By Anthropic. Good for longer texts and explanations. claude.ai

Age limits differ. Most services are for adults, or for teens with a parent’s consent. Check the terms of the service you pick. The order is alphabetical by company. Nobody pays us to recommend anything.

Stay safe

5 rules are all you need

You don’t need to understand the tech. These rules protect you with any AI tool.

🔒Nothing personalNever type passwords, PINs, card numbers, your Social Security number or medical records into a chat.
🔎Check what mattersFor health, money and official matters, check the answer with an expert or on an official website.
🧠You decideAI suggests, you decide. You are responsible for what you send or post.
📞Voices can lieAI can copy a voice and a face. If someone asks for money, always call back on a number you know.
⏸️No rush"Last chance" and "act now" are signs of a sales pitch or a scam, not good advice.
