How we verify

  1. We go to the original. The company's own announcement, the official documentation, the study or the law. Not a repost.
  2. We check the date. AI moves fast, so every news post says when we last confirmed it.
  3. We name the source. It's on the slide and in the caption, so you can check it yourself.
  4. We fix mistakes in public. If we get something wrong, we post a correction with the date and what changed.

No hype and no scare stories. If something is uncertain, we say so.
