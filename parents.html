Talk by age

How to talk with kids about AI

You don’t have to be an expert. What works best is playing a game together and asking one question afterwards. Here’s what kids understand at each age.

Ages 6–8AI learns from examples

Compare it with how your child learns. Good examples, good learning. Bad examples, and AI learns the wrong thing.

Add Ludko’s rule: "Only tell AI what you would tell a stranger on the street."

💬 What would Ludko think of a green apple if he had only ever seen red ones?
Ages 8–10AI guesses the next word

When AI writes, it keeps guessing which word fits best. Usually it gets it right. But even when it doesn’t know something, it still writes something.

That’s why it sounds sure even when it’s wrong. We check the important things.

💬 When AI tells you something, how do you find out if it’s true?
Ages 10–13Check it

Teach your child three questions. Who says so? What do other reliable sources say? Since when is it true?

With pictures, mistakes like odd fingers help. But only knowing where a picture comes from gives certainty.

💬 Which source do you trust, and why?
Ages 13+Deepfakes, scams and fairness

AI can copy a voice and a face. Scammers push on time, money and secrecy.

For homework, talk about where the line is. What does your child learn if AI writes the assignment?

💬 When AI writes for me, who is doing the learning?
What to allow

Yes, or better not?

AI doesn’t have to be banned at home. It depends on what your child uses it for. Younger kids should try it with you.

👍 Sure, go ahead

  • ❓Asking about things they’re curious about. For example why the sky is blue.
  • ✏️Making up poems and stories with AI.
  • 📘Getting an explanation of something they didn’t understand at school.
  • 👨‍👧Trying AI together with you and talking about it afterwards.

🤐 Better not

  • 🏠Typing their full name, school, address or family phone numbers into AI.
  • 🔑Giving away passwords. A password belongs to nobody else, AI included.
  • 📷Uploading photos of friends without asking them.
  • 📝Letting AI do the whole assignment for them.
What to watch out for

Five things worth talking about

🔒Privacy

A name and a school are enough to find out where a child can be found. Where you live, or that you’re away on vacation, doesn’t belong in any chat.

📷Photos

A photo belongs to the person in it. Without their permission your child doesn’t upload it anywhere. That goes for AI apps too.

📝Homework

AI can explain the material. But if it writes the whole essay, your child learns nothing. If AI helped, they should say so at school.

🎭Deepfakes

Someone can make a fake video of a classmate too. Your child shouldn’t share it. They should report it and tell you or a teacher.

🤖A chatbot as a "friend"

A chatbot is always nice and always has time. Remind your child that it’s a program and doesn’t think like a person. If something is bothering them, they should come to you.

AI Sanely · family agreement

How we use AI at home

  1. AI is our helper. But we do our own thinking.
  2. We don’t type names, addresses, schools, passwords or phone numbers into AI.
  3. We don’t upload photos of other people without their permission.
  4. We check important things in another source, or we ask.
  5. When AI helps us with homework, we say so.
  6. When something scares or confuses us, we talk about it at home. Nobody will be angry.
  7. We have a family password. If someone calls for help and wants money, we ask for it.
