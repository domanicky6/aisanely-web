How to play with the whole class

📽️Projector and A+. Turn on larger text in the game. Cards and buttons are big enough for the back row.
✋Voting. Before each answer, let the class vote with their hands (thumbs up/down, or A/B/C cards). Then click.
🗣️Read aloud. With first and second graders, you or a student read the questions aloud. Then the class votes.
⏸️Calm mode. In timed games, turn on calm mode. Leave time to think and talk.
🏆Teams. In Ludko to the top, groups compete to see who climbs with fewer slips.
❓“Why?” The best question after every answer. Even with a right answer, let students explain it in their own words.

AI Sanely · classroom agreement

How we use AI in our class

  1. We use AI as a helper, not as a replacement for our own thinking.
  2. When AI helped us, we say so: we write what it helped with and how.
  3. We check facts from AI in another reliable source.
  4. We don’t put personal data, passwords or classmates’ photos into AI.
  5. We don’t use AI pictures, videos or voices to hurt or make fun of anyone.
  6. When something from AI scares or confuses us, we tell a teacher or a parent.
  7. We are responsible for what we hand in or post.
