What your browser remembers

So you don’t have to start over, the site saves a few things right in your browser. The technical term is local storage. These aren’t cookies, they aren’t sent anywhere and we can’t see them. They stay there until you delete them.

  • ⭐Game progressStars, badges and finished games.
  • 🔊Game settingsSound, larger text and calm mode.
  • ✅Ticked-off stepsWhich of the 7 steps on the Start here page you’ve done.
  • 🔠Larger textWhether you turned on the A+ button.

PDFs via Instagram

When you comment a word like PROMPTS, we send you a PDF in an Instagram DM. All we see is what anyone on Instagram sees: your username, your comment and our conversation.

We don’t copy any of it anywhere else. We don’t add you to any list, we don’t sell anything and we don’t send you marketing messages afterwards.

Comments and messages are handled by Instagram (Meta) under its own rules. We also manage posts and replies through a social media management tool that works with them only on our behalf. You can delete your comment or message at any time. If you want us to delete the conversation too, just tell us.

Kids and schools

The games need no sign-up, have no chat and never ask for a name. Kids don’t have to fill in anything about themselves. In class you can run them on a projector without any account.

Where the site runs, and links to other sites

The site runs on GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub’s servers see your IP address when you visit and use it for security and operations. We have no access to that data.

In some places we link to other websites: Instagram, news sources or tools for schools. Once you click a link, their rules apply.

Counting visits

We want to know which pages and games people enjoy. So we count visits with GoatCounter. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t store anything in your browser.

A visit sends: the page address, the site you came from and your screen width. We also count some clicks, for example on Instagram, on our email address or starting a game.

GoatCounter works out your country from your IP address, and your browser name and system from its user agent. It doesn’t store the IP address or the user agent in its database. To avoid counting one visit twice, it keeps them in memory for at most 8 hours.

We only see totals, for example how many people started Ludko to the top today. We don’t know who you are. We don’t sell anything and don’t pass it on to anyone.

Counting only happens on aisanely.com. If your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on, nothing is sent.
